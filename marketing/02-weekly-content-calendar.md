## Weekly rotation of content angles

Cycle these four angles so weeks don't repeat:

1. **Money week** — costs, pricing comparisons, "what X actually costs"
2. **Visibility week** — traces, tool calls, "what your agent actually did"
3. **Multi-agent week** — comparing agents, unified dashboard, new agent support
4. **Community week** — contributor spotlights, user dashboards, roadmap input

## Evergreen content bank (use when nothing shipped)

- "Show your dashboard" screenshot + ask others to share theirs
- One supported agent spotlight (35+ to choose from — a year of Tuesdays)
- A surprising stat from your own usage data
- Re-share your best-performing post from 4+ weeks ago with new framing
- Answer a recurring Discord/GitHub question publicly

## Rules

- **Explain, don't hype.** Write like a human helping someone understand — plain sentences, first person ("here's how I…"), no dramatic hooks or punchline framing. State the numbers, then explain simply why they matter. (Hemanth's standing preference, 2026-07-06.)
- Every post has an image, GIF, or chart. Text-only posts underperform 3–5× for tool accounts.
- Feature Friday is non-negotiable — it's the heartbeat that builds "this project ships" reputation. If nothing user-facing shipped, post progress ("this week: 9 PRs, what's coming next").
- Track in `drafts/`: Cowork writes `drafts/YYYY-MM-DD.md` per day; move posted items to `drafts/posted/` (or just delete).
