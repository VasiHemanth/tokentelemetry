### LinkedIn — Cost-visibility post (longer)

> Most AI cost dashboards tell you "tokens used." That's not the number that matters.
>
> What matters: which credit pool actually got charged, and whether the number on screen is a real bill or an API-list-price equivalent on a flat subscription.
>
> Two things TokenTelemetry added recently that changed how I read my own data:
>
> **1. Chip-aware local power.** Sessions on Ollama/llama.cpp/vLLM are priced by electricity. The dashboard now reads your actual chip (M-series, etc.) and applies real wattage — not a placeholder. For a Mac Studio running Qwen sessions all day, the difference is meaningful.
>
> **2. Drain-priority billing.** Expand any agent and see its credit pool order: interactive vs. programmatic, Claude's separate Agent-SDK pool (the one with no auto-fallback), pool sizes, and $0-stop warnings. Suddenly a session cost that looked wrong makes sense.

> Both read entirely from local logs. No SDK, no cloud, no code changes.
