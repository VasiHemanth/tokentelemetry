## LinkedIn — Cost-visibility post (longer version of X insight)

> **Most teams don't know their AI coding agents draw from more than one billing pool.**
>
> If your team uses Claude Code, here's something worth knowing: the interactive app and the Agent SDK (`claude -p`, SDK integrations, CI pipelines) draw from *separate* credit pools. The SDK pool has no auto-fallback — if it hits zero, your CI runs and scripted agent jobs just start failing, with no warning baked into the billing UI.
>
> Same story for Codex, Gemini, Copilot and Cursor — each has its own bucket priority for how usage gets billed.
>
> We just shipped this visibility into TokenTelemetry (open source, runs 100% locally on your machine, zero config): expand any agent under Settings and see its drain order, pool sizes, and a stops-at-$0 warning — split by interactive vs. programmatic use.
>
> If you're responsible for AI tooling spend on a team, this is the kind of thing you want visible before it surprises you in a CI failure at 2am.
>
