## X / Twitter — Tuesday Tip

**Feature spotlight:** Which credit pool is your agent actually draining?

Most Claude users don't realise Claude has *two separate credit pools* — one for interactive use, one for programmatic/SDK/CI use (including `claude -p` and any SDK calls). They don't share budget and the programmatic pool has no auto-fallback — it stops at $0 with no warning. TokenTelemetry now shows you exactly which pool each agent drains, and flags when you're close to the edge.
